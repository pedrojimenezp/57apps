Privacy Policy for Heads Up

Last updated: August 17, 2026

Heads Up ("the app") is developed by 57Apps ("we", "us"). This policy explains how the app handles your information.

Summary

Heads Up does not collect, transmit, or share any of your personal data. All information you enter into the app stays on your device.

Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect, store, or have access to:

  • Your tasks, reminders, or any content you enter into the app
  • Your name, email, or any account information (the app does not require an account)
  • Analytics, usage statistics, or crash reports
  • Advertising identifiers
  • Location data

Information Stored on Your Device

The app stores the following data locally on your device only, using standard local storage (SQLite database and device preferences):

  • The tasks and reminders you create, including due dates, priorities, and completion status
  • App settings and preferences

This data is never transmitted off your device. Uninstalling the app permanently deletes this data.

Permissions

The app requests the following device permissions, used only to power its reminder features:

  • Notifications — to show you reminders for tasks that are due.
  • Exact alarms / schedule exact alarm — to ensure reminders fire at the precise time you set, rather than being delayed by the system.
  • Foreground service — to keep a persistent "due today" reminder visible until you complete or postpone the task.
  • Receive boot completed — to reschedule your reminders after your device restarts.

None of these permissions are used to collect or transmit data; they exist solely to deliver reminders you've scheduled within the app.

Third Parties

Heads Up does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. No data is shared with any third party because no data leaves your device.

Children's Privacy

Heads Up does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children, because it does not collect information at all.

Changes to This Policy

This policy describes the app as it works today: fully local, with no accounts and no data leaving your device. We plan to add optional sync in a future version (for example, via a server we operate or a cloud storage provider like Google Drive) so you can access your tasks across devices. That feature will be opt-in, and we will update this policy — describing exactly what is sent, where, and why — before it ships, along with revising the "Last updated" date above.

Contact
